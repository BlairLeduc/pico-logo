# P13 M0 clock sweep — Plus 2 W, 2026-08-23

**The present figures in this sweep are void above 150 MHz.** `clk_peri` does
not follow `clk_sys`: `set_sys_clock_pll` parks it on the USB PLL at 48 MHz
whenever the system PLL moves, so `spi_set_baudrate(75 MHz)` could only deliver
24 MHz and the LCD wire went from 16.4 ms to 51.2. Fixed the same day with
`PICO_CLOCK_ADJUST_PERI_CLOCK_WITH_SYS_CLOCK=1`; the sweep wants re-running.

Everything else in the sweep is good, and the body figures are the point.

| clock | arith stmt | body @ 3 obj | present (measured) | present (should be) |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 150 MHz | 52.5 µs | 45.9 ms | 19.6 ms | 19.6 ms |
| 200 | 39.5 | 34.4 | **58.95** | 27.0 |
| 250 | 31.5 | 27.3 | **59.45** | 21.6 |
| 300 | 25.5 | 22.6 | **57.4** | 18.0 |

**The interpreter is purely clock-bound.** Speedups of 1.000 / 1.329 / 1.667 /
2.059 against clock ratios of 1.000 / 1.333 / 1.667 / 2.000 — inside 3 % at
every point, which also proves `ticks` stayed honest across the sweep.

**The die barely warms**: 28.2 → 23.9 °C at 150 (cooling from a previous run),
24.1 → 24.8 at 200, 24.8 → 25.7 at 250, 25.7 → 26.6 at 300. Thermals are not a
constraint at any of these clocks.

**The SPI dividers are coarse and it matters.** clk_peri divides by an even
prescale times a post-divider, so only clocks reaching 75 MHz exactly keep the
display at full speed: 150 → 75 (÷2) and 300 → 75 (÷4), but 200 → 50 and
250 → 62.5. **An overclock to 200 makes the interpreter 1.33× faster and the
display 1.5× slower.**
